ROOTBOUND: grow a living root through seven depths — drink deep water, split around stone, bloom against the Stone Heart. You steer a growing root tip through the earth (mouse and/or WASD/arrows). The root grows continuously; you steer it. Collect water droplets to grow longer and score; hitting stone costs health; grubs eat roots; a branch bud splits off a second autopilot tip; Space is a deep-tap speed burst. 7 named depths (Topsoil, Clay, Gravel, Sand, Chalk, Bedrock, The Deep), each with different hazard density. Boss at the bottom: the Stone Heart, a rock mass with 3 glowing weak veins — crack all 3 to bloom and win. Lose when health hits zero. HUD: depth name + meter, water, health, length, score, burst meter. Start/title screen, pause, win/loss results screen with restart. Palette: dark earth with glowing green/gold root. Synthesized WebAudio only, mute toggle. 2D canvas, no library, no network.
